# Owner approval checklist

This concept is intentionally polished while keeping uncertain business information visible. Before publishing it as an official website, confirm the following with the owner.

## Identity and contact

- [ ] Official business/studio name and spelling.
- [ ] Approved logo, wordmark, colours and typography.
- [ ] Whether the listed phone number `+91 94669 40493` is the correct public contact and WhatsApp number.
- [ ] Full address, map pin and directions wording.
- [ ] Approved email address, if one should be published.
- [ ] Office hours or a decision not to publish them.
- [ ] Domain name and preferred canonical URL.

## Services and claims

- [ ] Which proposed services are actually offered.
- [ ] Correct names and scope for architectural planning, glass/façade work, interiors, visualization, material studies and consultation.
- [ ] Professional responsibilities, registrations and statutory/consultant boundaries.
- [ ] Approved process stages, deliverables, fees and next-step language.
- [ ] Any claims about experience, awards or qualifications supported by evidence.

## Portfolio and content

- [ ] Replace every illustrative SVG with approved project photography, drawings or models.
- [ ] Confirm project names, locations, client permissions, dates and authorship.
- [ ] Remove any concept study that does not represent approved work.
- [ ] Review each article for authorship, publication date and final wording.
- [ ] Replace the provisional About narrative with approved studio information.
- [ ] Confirm whether social profiles, testimonials or reviews may be displayed.

## Forms, privacy and launch

- [ ] Choose an approved email or CRM delivery provider.
- [ ] Configure secrets only in the deployment environment, never in public files.
- [ ] Confirm consent wording, data retention, access and deletion process.
- [ ] Replace the draft privacy policy and terms with reviewed text.
- [ ] Decide whether analytics, cookies or third-party embeds are required.
- [ ] Set `noIndex` to `false` only after the content, domain and legal pages are approved.
- [ ] Update robots/sitemap behavior for the approved production domain.

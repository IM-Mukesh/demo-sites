# Asset provenance

## Scope

The files in `public/brand/` and `public/images/architect/` were created locally for the premium architecture demo. The brand marks and social preview are original SVG artwork for the provisional **ARCHITECT** identity. The architectural scenes are original illustrative concept artwork, drawn as lightweight SVG compositions for this demo.

The concept scenes use a shared visual language: warm ivory paper tones, charcoal structural planes, pale stone volumes, muted olive planting, and restrained window/light geometry. They are intentionally generic studies rather than documentation of a built work.

## Image status

The files in `public/images/architect/` are **illustrative concept artwork only**. They do not depict real projects, commissioned photography, or licensed photography, and should not be presented as photographs or as completed work by an architecture practice.

## Launch requirement

Before launch, replace the illustrative concept artwork with approved photography or other approved project imagery. Confirm usage rights, project names, credits, and alt text for every replacement image before publishing.
